Frequently asked questions

When will Display & Cell Pros fully reopen? +

Our full relaunch, including the online storefront, is set for February 1, 2027. Until then we're running on limited hours while we restructure our inventory.

What are your hours right now? +

During limited operations we're open Wednesday through Friday, 10 AM to 6 PM, and closed other days. Call or text ahead and we'll do our best to work with your schedule.

What services can I get right now? +

Diagnostics, software troubleshooting, and general IT support are fully available. Screen repairs are available too, depending on the model.

How do I know if you have a screen for my device? +

Call or text us at (509) 255-3852 with your device's make and model, and we'll check stock for you. If we don't have it on hand, we'll let you know when it can be ordered in.

Can I still book a screen repair if my model isn't in stock? +

Yes. Reach out and we'll walk you through the options and timing for your specific model.

What devices and brands do you work on? +

We repair phones and displays and now service game consoles, and our IT support covers computers and everyday tech. Send us your make and model and we'll confirm we can help.

How long will my repair take? +

Turnaround depends on the repair and whether the part is in stock. We'll give you a time estimate before any work begins.

How much will it cost? +

Pricing depends on your device and the repair needed. You'll get a clear quote up front, and nothing is done without your OK.

Do you offer any discounts? +

Yes. Tribal members receive a 20% discount. Just mention it when you contact us.

Do you accept walk-ins? +

We recommend calling or texting ahead during limited hours so we can confirm parts and make sure someone is ready for you.

Do you come to me? +

Yes, mobile service is available around the Spokane area for a $25 trip fee. Contact us to check if your location is covered.

What should I bring when I drop off a device? +

Bring the device, its charger if it has trouble powering on, and a description of the problem. If you can, back up your data first and turn off Find My or device locks if we ask you to.

Is my data safe during a repair? +

We only access what's needed to diagnose and test the repair, and we never browse your personal files. We always recommend backing up your device beforehand.

What kind of IT support do you offer? +

We help both individuals and small businesses with setup, troubleshooting, software issues, and general tech questions. Ask us whether your issue can be handled remotely or needs a visit.

What about warranties on repairs? +

Questions about a past repair or warranty coverage? Contact Ryan directly and we'll look into it right away.

What if my device is already in for repair? +

Repairs already in progress keep going as normal, and we'll contact you directly with updates. Feel free to reach out anytime for a status check.

How do I pay? +

We'll confirm accepted payment options when you book. Online payments will return with the full storefront on February 1, 2027.

Can I sign up to hear when you're fully back? +

Yes, use the Notify Me box above or follow up with us by phone or email.

How do I reach Ryan with a question? +

Call or text (509) 255-3852, or email ryan@displaycellpros.com. Ryan answers personally.
