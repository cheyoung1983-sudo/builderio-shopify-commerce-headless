01 • Authoritative Reference Layer

Federal Data Constraints & The 2026 BIA Registry

Individual tribal enrollment databases are maintained independently by sovereign tribal nations and shielded from external queries; even federal Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) Certificate of Degree of Indian Blood (CDIB) requests require 4 to 12 months of regional processing. Consequently, compliant verification systems seed an authoritative registry with the 575 federally recognized entities published on January 30, 2026, while verifying individual credentials through document intelligence and human review.

Credential Standardization Across 575 Tribes

WHTI ETC Gap

Only approximately 12 to 15 federally recognized tribes issue Western Hemisphere Travel Initiative (WHTI) Enhanced Tribal Cards (ETCs) with RFID and Machine Readable Zones (MRZs). Roughly 97.6% of recognized tribes issue standard ID cards or official enrollment letters lacking border-grade MRZs.

Architectural Takeaway: Relying solely on automated MRZ barcode readers would reject ~97.6% of legitimate tribal members. Pairing AI document extraction with human review ensures inclusive verification.

Privacy vs. Audit Trade-Off

Data Retention Lifecycle Segregation

Retaining raw government ID images and unmasked tribal enrollment numbers for five years introduces unnecessary data-breach liability. Immediately upon human Case approval and cryptographic hash logging, the workflow invokes the KYC provider's redaction endpoint (DELETE /api/v1/verifications/:id) to purge raw ID images within 24 hours while locking statutory tax certificates and carrier proofs of delivery for 60 months (5 years).

  • 🗑️ Raw GovID & Tribal Card Images: Purged immediately post-review (≤ 1 day).
  • 🔄 Address Exemption Cache: Invalidated immediately on any address change.
  • 🔒 Tax Certificates & Carrier PODs: Locked 60 months (WAC 458-20-192).

05 • Multi-State Revenue Compliance

State Statutory Tax Rules & 2026 Fraud Guardrails

Because state revenue departments enforce distinct territorial, residency, and title-passage conditions for on-reservation sales, exemption rules must be checked state by state. This guide covers Washington only.

⚠️ IRS Anti-Fraud Compliance (IR-2026-112, Sept 18, 2026)

The IRS issued an official warning against promoters marketing fake "Tribal Tax Credits" (reportable on IRS Form 14242). Public-facing invoices, database fields, and checkout labels must explicitly classify Track 1 as a commercial retail price discount and Track 2 as state retail sales tax preemption—never a federal tax credit.

🔄 Address-Change & Credential Safeguards

Under WAC 458-20-192, any change in delivery address invalidates an existing exemption statement. Comparing a cryptographic hash of the normalized shipping address at checkout automatically triggers fresh PostGIS boundary verification and certificate renewal, while unique credential hash indexing prevents multi-account sharing.
