01 • Federal Reference

The 2026 BIA List of Federally Recognized Tribes

Tribal enrollment records are generally kept by each tribal nation and generally can't be looked up online. That is why a person on our team reviews membership documents by hand. For reference, the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) published its current list of 575 federally recognized tribes on January 30, 2026.

Federal Register 91 FR 4102

Doc 2026-01899

The January 30, 2026 BIA list includes 575 tribal entities in the contiguous 48 states and Alaska. It adds the Lumbee Tribe of North Carolina and includes some name changes.

Lumbee Tribe of North Carolina ADDED

Recognized by the FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act, enacted Dec 18, 2025 (Pub. L. 119-60 Sec. 8803), with conditions that include Secretarial verification of the tribe's roll and a delay in federal services.

04 • Washington Sales Tax

Washington Rules & Fake Tax Credit Warnings

Sales tax rules for deliveries to reservations differ from state to state. This guide covers Washington only.

State & Rule Form Who May Qualify Delivery At Checkout
Washington (WA)
WAC 458-20-192 		A signed Washington tax exemption certificate is generally required. Generally, an enrolled member (or, in some cases, a member's spouse) of the tribe whose reservation receives the delivery. Delivery generally must be within that tribe's reservation. A new delivery address may need a new form. Tax charged; refund possible after we confirm eligibility
⚠️ IRS Warning on Fake “Tribal Tax Credits” (IR-2026-112, Sept 18, 2026)

The IRS warned about promoters selling “Tribal Tax Credits” that do not exist under federal law, and asks people to report suspected schemes on IRS Form 14242. Our 20% member discount is a store price discount, and any Washington refund is a state sales tax matter. Neither one is a federal tax credit.

🔄 If Your Delivery Address Changes

Under WAC 458-20-192, a change of delivery address may affect whether an exemption applies. Contact us before ordering to a new address, and we'll tell you whether a new form is needed.
